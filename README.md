# 🥷 Ninja Mission Planner

Welcome to the **Ninja Mission Planner**, a Swift+SwiftUI-powered app that lets you organize your life like a shadowy master of productivity. This is the official sample app for my course _**App Building with Swift: Your Beginner’s Guide**_, and it’s designed to teach you how to build real apps for real people — even if your only previous programming experience was summoning Pikachu in Super Smash Bros.

If you're brand new to coding, don't worry. You're exactly where you're supposed to be. This app is your dojo — a safe place to learn, experiment, and become the kind of Swift ninja who writes code with confidence and maybe a touch of elegance.

---

## Overview

The **Ninja Mission Planner** is a cooler, more focused version of a traditional ToDo app — but built for future app devs like you. Imagine a place where you can add your epic quests (aka “missions”), mark them as complete, organize them into categories like Fitness, Learning, Personal Projects, and Work... all while looking like you know exactly what you're doing. Because soon, you will.

Unlike most ToDo apps, this one isn’t tied to any not-free backend or login system you don’t understand yet. Everything runs **locally on your device**, which means less stress, more magic, and code you can actually read and learn from.

Oh, and it’s all written in Swift, using SwiftUI. You’ll see real-world app architecture, UI building, state management, and more — all while learning to master the basics of iOS development.

## ✨ Features

### 👤 Authentication (But Make It Local)
- **Sign Up** with your ninja alias and pick your emoji avatar.
- **Sign In** to your custom profile and plan your day like a stealthy planner.
- **Log Out** anytime to simulate user flows or start fresh.

### 🎯 Mission Control
- **Add Missions** for anything you’re working on — workouts, side projects, work goals, or learning spells (I mean, skills).
- **Categorize Missions** into one of four training disciplines: `Fitness`, `Learning`, `Personal Projects`, and `Work`.
- **Mark Missions as Complete** — because every ninja loves crossing things off.
- **Swipe to Delete** or re-prioritize with ease.

### 🔍 Filtering
- Want to see only your `Learning` goals or just focus on `Work` today? Filter missions by category with a flick of your wrist (okay, a tap — but still cool).

### 🧙‍♂️ Custom Profile
- **Edit your profile** anytime and change your ninja identity (emoji included).
- Enable or disable **visual effects** — like glowing ninja auras and jumping ninjas — because why not?

### 🧠 Built with Beginners in Mind
- Uses **local storage** only — no databases, no APIs, no stress.
- All written in **Swift + SwiftUI** — beginner-friendly and Apple-approved.
- Fully readable, fully functional, and built to teach the key concepts of Swift development, like:
  - Views and modifiers  
  - Data binding  
  - State management  
  - Navigation  
  - Local persistence  
  - MVVM pattern  
  - And more

## Configuring and running the project

👀 If you want to run the app in Simluator, you do *not* need to select any Team under Signing & Capabilities ✅

To configure the Ninja Mission Planner app without an Apple Developer account, follow these steps:

1. In the Ninja Mission Planner target's Signing & Capabilities panes click Add Account, and log in with your Apple ID.
2. Chose Your Name (Personal Team) from the team menu for the Ninja Mission Planner target.
3. Build and run your app.
4. On iOS and iPadOS devices navigate to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and trust your developer certificate.

If you have an Apple Developer account, then use your Developer Account credentials for Step 1
